## M8205(B) manual needed

_WBS TECHNICAL SUPPORT · Jun 20, 2012|45_

> The mic-pre module is not really a mic-pre you would want to use for any sort of music. Its intent was for talk-back facility in the machine room to the control room - more of a courtesy thing so the technicians could communicate with out having to have a MicroCom system or other intercom set-up. So to be VERY CLEAR it was not meant for any sort of programme.
>
> I have a frame of them somewhere here I believe..... BUt I have the brochure.
>
> I quote "The M8206 Maxtrixing Distribution Amplifier may be utilized for those stereo applications where the Left and Right channels need to be combined as L + L (difference) signals. This amplifier has two inputs, each with its own sensitivity control, and six isolated 600 ohm outputs."
>
> Catering to the american market dictated the spelling!!!
>
> A basic DA was 1 in 8 out M8205.
>
> +30 dB to -6dB cont. variable Bridging Gain (per channel in the 8206)
> Freq Res - +-0.1 dB from 15 Hz to 25 kHz
> input - 30k or greater balanced
> output - 600 ohm or less, balanced
> Common Mode Rejection - >80 dB, 20 - 20 kHz
>
> +-22 volts DC 45 mA idling 145 mA at max output
>
> THere ya go!
>
> OH if I really look at the picture:
> -22v = pin Q/13
> gnd = pin R/14
> +22v = pin S/15
>
> Inputs are of course A/1, and B/2
>
> You can read the rest from the card it self.
>
> Hope this helps!! - or did I go off on another rant/tangent?
